Course overview

The MSc Business (International Management) at the University of Nottingham Malaysia (UNM) offers a transformative learning experience, providing students from diverse disciplines with advanced knowledge and practical skills in business and international management. This one-year, full-time programme is designed to prepare students for leadership roles in the global business landscape.

Accredited by the EFMD Quality Improvement System (EQUIS), Association to Advance Collegiate Schools of Business (AACSB) and Association of MBAs (AMBA), the programme combines core modules in finance, business analytics, and global human resource management with specialised studies in international business strategy and innovation. The modular structure allows students to tailor their learning through electives and a final project, choosing between an academic dissertation or a business-focused project.

Students gain expertise in global market dynamics, strategic decision-making, and business analytics, while exploring ethics, sustainability, and corporate social responsibility. Practical learning through group projects, case studies, and interactive sessions ensures graduates are equipped to address real-world challenges.

 

Why Choose This Course?

  • Global Business Focus: Gain advanced knowledge of international business strategies, cultural diversity, and global market dynamics for thriving in the global economy.
  • Specialised Skill Development: Master critical skills in business analytics, leadership, strategic management, and problem-solving tailored to international management.
  • Flexible Study Options: Choose between academic and business routes to align with your career goals, including a business project or a research dissertation.
  • Industry-Relevant Curriculum: Accredited by EQUIS, AACSB, and AMBA, the programme is offered by a PRME Champion School, integrating real-world applications, sustainability principles, and ethical business practices aligned with the United Nations' Principles for Responsible Management Education (PRME).
  • Enhanced Employability: Graduates are equipped with expertise in managing global operations, decision-making, and navigating dynamic business environments.
  • Accredited Programme: Delivered by a globally recognised business school, accredited by EFMD Quality Improvement System (EQUIS), ensuring academic excellence and industry relevance.

Course content

One year full-time programme comprising of six compulsory modules in Semester 1 and 2. In Semester 3 students complete a dissertation or business project (with presentation). Teaching comprises lectures, some small group teaching lab work and seminars.

Core modules

  • Managing Organisations and Markets 
  • Employability & Global Human Resources Management
  • Business Analytics and Research Methods  

Core modules

  • Managing Financial Resources
  • International Business Strategy
  • Managing Innovation and Entrepreneurship
  • Consulting Skills

Core modules

  • Business Dissertation/Business Project

Entry requirements

All candidates are considered on an individual basis and we accept a broad range of qualifications. The entrance requirements below apply to 2026 entry.

A relevant second-class honours degree (2:2), or a relevant professional qualification deemed equivalent to a first degree with honours, preferably in business studies or management. Non-UK qualifications will be assessed against this standard.

Applicants without a degree in business studies or management must demonstrate that they have completed equivalent modules in the relevant discipline.

Before starting an online application, applicants are required to carefully read, review, and understand the Compliance with MQA Standards and Programme Standards, as well as the Checklist for application.

Applicants must possess a degree awarded by a recognised university.

Faculty-Specific Requirements

  • Faculty of Science and Engineering: A qualification in a related field is usually necessary for admission to postgraduate programmes.
  • Faculty of Arts and Social Sciences: Consideration may be given to those whose previous degree is in a different discipline if they have relevant work experience and can supply further supporting documents as outlined in the application checklist.

In instances where additional evaluation is required, applicants may be invited to participate in an interview.

These guidelines are intended as a general reference; eligibility is confirmed following submission and assessment of a complete application.

IELTS (Academic):

6.5 (with no less than 6.0 in each element)

TOEFL (iBT):

 90 (minimum 19 in Writing and Listening, 20 in Reading and 22 in Speaking)

PTE (Academic):

71 (with no less than 65 in each element)
MUET: Band 4.5
  • IELTS (Academic), TOEFL iBT, and PTE (Academic) test results must be less than two years old.
  • IELTS One Skill Retake is accepted.
  • IELTS Academic Online, TOEFL iBT Home Edition, and PTE Academic Online are not accepted.
  • MUET results are valid for five years from the release date.
  • English language tests must be taken at a test centre situated in the applicant’s country of residence.
  • International applicants who meet the University’s requirements may also need to submit extra documents or an approved English test for MOHE, if required.

Learning and assessment

How you will learn

  • Lectures
  • Seminars
  • Tutorials
  • Workshops
  • Field trips
  • Supervision
  • Computer labs
  • Lab sessions

In addition to lectures, some modules have lab sessions, some have workshops and some drop in sessions. Each module is run with the aim of providing best learning experience for students and module objectives are achieved by devising the most appropriate delivery and assessment methods.

How you will be assessed

  • Coursework
  • Group coursework
  • Dissertation
  • Examinations
  • Presentation
  • Essay

Modules are assessed by a combination of exams and coursework at the end of the relevant semester.
