Postgraduate Study

Please add a keyword and select a level of study.

At the University of Nottingham Malaysia, have your pick of postgraduate programmes that meet the demands of cutting-edge industries as well as provide an outlet for endless curiosity.

From engineering to biotechnology, finance to psychology, international relations to business management – you would be hard-pressed to find a more diverse and refined array of offerings. With a distinctively global outlook, our postgraduate programmes are designed to provide you with advanced knowledge that is highly valued by employers not only in Malaysia, but across the world. 

You will have access to faculty members with many years of experience in industry as well as state-of-the-art academic facilities equipped with the latest technology and resources.

FAQs

What is the programme structure for postgraduate programmes at the University of Nottingham Malaysia?
Programme structure varies depending on the subject. However, in most postgraduate programmes, you will be exposed to a well-rounded combination of lectures, seminars, and practical or research work. Some courses may also provide opportunities for internships or work placements.
What are my career prospects after completing postgraduate programmes at the University of Nottingham Malaysia?
You can expect to have a competitive edge in the job market, including but certainly not limited to eligibility for higher-level positions in your chosen field. Furthermore, the global outlook and comprehensive nature of our programmes will enable you to carry this advantage abroad as well.
What support services are available to me as a postgraduate student at the University of Nottingham Malaysia?
We provide a wide range of support services, including academic and learning support, counselling and mental health services, and career guidance.
As a postgraduate student, can I participate in research projects at the University of Nottingham Malaysia?
Yes, you certainly can under the guidance of experienced faculty members. In fact, we encourage it.
What study abroad opportunities are available to me as a postgraduate student at the University of Nottingham Malaysia?
You will have a variety of study abroad opportunities, including through exchange programmes with UNM campuses in the UK and China and some other universities around the world. These programmes will give you the chance to experience different cultures and broaden your horizons – so be sure to make good use of them.
Is financial aid available to me as a postgraduate student at the University of Nottingham Malaysia?
Yes, we are proud to provide a range of financial aid options to eligible students primarily in the form of scholarships.
