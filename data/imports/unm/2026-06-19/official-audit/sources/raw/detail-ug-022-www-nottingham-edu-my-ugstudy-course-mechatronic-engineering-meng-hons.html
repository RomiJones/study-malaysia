Course overview

The Master of Engineering with Honours in Mechatronic Engineering at the University of Nottingham Malaysia (UNM) integrates mechanical, electrical, electronic, and computer engineering to create innovative, intelligent systems. This four-year programme equips students with advanced skills in automation, robotics, and control engineering, preparing them to tackle complex engineering challenges in modern industries. With a strong emphasis on project-based learning, students engage in hands-on group and individual projects throughout their studies, fostering creativity and technical expertise.

Recognised by the Institution of Engineering and Technology (IET) and the Engineering Accreditation Council (EAC) Malaysia, the programme meets the academic requirements for Chartered Engineer status, offering global professional credibility. Students benefit from innovative facilities and expert instruction in artificial intelligence, digital signal processing, and robotics. Ranked 100th globally for Engineering Technology (QS World University Rankings by Subject 2024), UNM delivers innovation-driven education, preparing graduates for impactful careers in automation, aerospace, and manufacturing.

Why choose this course?

  • Interdisciplinary Focus: Combines mechanical, electrical, and computer engineering, equipping students with expertise in automation, robotics, and intelligent systems.
  • Practical Learning: Engage in practical group and individual projects from Year 1, fostering real-world problem-solving and innovation.
  • Global Recognition: Accredited by IET and EAC Malaysia, meeting requirements for Chartered Engineer status and ensuring international career pathways.
  • Industry-Relevant Modules: Includes cutting-edge topics like artificial intelligence, renewable technologies, and digital signal processing, aligned with future industry demands.
  • Career-Ready Graduates: Develop technical, leadership, and teamwork skills, preparing for roles in advanced manufacturing, aerospace, and high-tech industries.

Entry requirements

All candidates are considered on an individual basis and we accept a broad range of qualifications. The entrance requirements below apply to 2026 entry.

A Level BBC, including Mathematics and Physics, excluding Critical Thinking and General Studies.
IB Diploma 26 points with 5 and 4 at Higher Level, including either Mathematics: Analysis and Approaches (HL) or Mathematics: Applications and Interpretation (HL), plus Physics (HL).
STPM BBC+, including Mathematics and Physics, excluding Pengajian Am.
UEC 5 B3s including Mathematics and Physics, excluding Bahasa Malaysia and Chinese language.
Australian Year 12 ATAR 82. Applicants are required to meet specific grade standards in Mathematics and Physics, aligned with A Level subject requirements. To fulfil these A Level standards, specific grade equivalents in the Senior Secondary Certificate of Education (SSCE) are required. For WACE, applicants must have studied both Mathematics Methods and Mathematics Specialist. For SACE, both Mathematical Methods and Specialist Mathematics are required. For other states and territories, the mathematics subjects will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis.
Ontario Secondary School Diploma (OSSD) 77% average based on 6 subjects, including Advanced Functions, Calculus and Vectors, and Physics. Applicants must meet specific grade requirements for subjects required at A Level: 75% for C and 81% for B.

Canadian Secondary School Diplomas from other provinces are acceptable and will be assessed based on the University’s requirements.
Gaokao (NCEE) and Senior High School Diploma (China) A 70% average in the Gaokao, including at least two required subjects (Mathematics and Physics) with scores of 75% and 73%, respectively. In addition, a minimum of 78% overall in the Senior High School Diploma is required, including at least two required subjects (Mathematics and Physics) with scores of 80% and 70%, respectively.

Gaokao scores are converted from 750 to a percentage scale.
Senior School Certificate Examination (CBSE) / Indian School Certificate (CISCE) - Class XII 78% average from the best four subjects, including Mathematics and Physics at 80% and 70%, respectively. CBSE Applied Mathematics is not accepted to meet the Mathematics requirement. Excludes local language, Environmental Education, General Studies, and Physical Education.
Advance Placement (AP) 4, 4, 3, including AP Calculus and AP Physics.
Diploma - Local Institutions Entry to Year 2 is assessed on a case-by-case basis, typically requiring a minimum GPA of 3.10 out of 4.0 and good grades in relevant subjects, including Mathematics and Physics.
Foundation - Local Institutions Acceptance is at the School’s discretion and requires a minimum GPA of 3.10 out of 4.0 and good grades in relevant subjects, including Mathematics and Physics.
Nottingham's Foundation Programme Successful completion is required, with a minimum score of 45% in all Mathematics and Physics modules.

Foundation progression options

It builds a strong academic foundation while enhancing language, critical thinking, and study skills. Students can also explore elective modules tailored to their chosen degree pathway.
