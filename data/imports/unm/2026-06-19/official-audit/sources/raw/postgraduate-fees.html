Postgraduate and Research Tuition Fees For 2026

Please note that the fees listed below are from January 2025 onwards.

 

Tuition Fees By Area of Study For Postgraduate Programmes

At the University of Nottingham Malaysia, tuition fees for Postgraduate Taught programmes are charged on a per-programme basis, while Postgraduate Research degrees are charged on a per-year basis. Payment plans vary for full-time and part-time students, with part-time students required to pay half the annual fee.

The fees listed below apply to applicants enrolling for the 2026 intake. For confirmation of your final tuition fees, please refer to your official offer letter.

FAQs

Do the tuition fees change every academic year?
The fees listed on the website are for the intake year of 2025/26. It is possible that the fees may change for different academic years. It is advisable to refer to your offer letter or contact the university administration for the most accurate information​.
Are there additional costs apart from the tuition fee?
This page primarily lists tuition fees. However, students may incur additional costs related to accommodation, textbooks, or other programme materials. It is, therefore, recommended to budget for these extra costs.
Is there a difference in tuition fees between taught and research postgraduate programme?
Yes, the Postgraduate Taught programmes are charged on a per programme basis (which is a one-time complete payment for the programme). However, for the Postgraduate Research programmes, the fee is charged on a per-year basis​.
Does the University of Nottingham Malaysia offer scholarships or financial aid that can help cover the tuition fee?
Yes, UNM offers scholarships and financial assistance to deserving students.
Does the master’s degree fee include accommodation at the University of Nottingham Malaysia campus?
The tuition fee covers the academic costs associated with a programme. Accommodation is separate and not included in the tuition fee. You can refer to the Accommodation section of the website for more information about housing costs and options.
What additional financial considerations should I be aware of as an international student?
International students should consider additional costs such as visa and immigration fees, travel costs, health insurance, and potential exchange rate fluctuations when budgeting for their studies.
