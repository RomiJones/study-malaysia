The Master of Science in Engineering (MSc) at Taylor’s University is a research degree designed for aspiring researchers and professionals who seek to push the boundaries of engineering knowledge. The programme is dedicated to fostering academic excellence, encouraging innovation, and developing graduates who demonstrate leadership, professionalism, and a strong sense of social responsibility.

 

Through advanced study, you will build critical thinking, analytical reasoning, and problem-solving capabilities that are essential in today’s complex engineering landscape. With a strong emphasis on research and applied practice, you will learn to address real-world engineering challenges and develop innovative solutions that contribute meaningfully to society, industry, and academia.

NEXT: PROGRAMME STRUCTURE

Our MSc in Engineering attracts highly motivated individuals from diverse backgrounds who are drawn by the programme’s strong reputation for academic excellence, innovation, and practical impact. Successful applicants gain the opportunity to work alongside experienced faculty, industry experts, and active research communities — making the programme both intellectually stimulating and deeply rewarding.

 

Through a thoughtfully designed curriculum, you will develop advanced competencies in project management, collaborative research, and technical communication. These skills prepare you not only to excel in your chosen area of specialisation but also to embrace lifelong learning and adapt to the rapid technological transformations shaping the future of engineering.

 

The Master of Science in Engineering blends advanced theoretical knowledge with hands-on application, allowing you to tailor your studies to align with your professional interests and career ambitions.

 

Programme Highlights:

  • Theoretical Knowledge: Deepen your understanding of advanced engineering concepts and research methodologies.
  • Laboratory Work: Apply theoretical insights through hands-on experiments in industry-standard facilities.
  • Independent Research: Conduct a thesis project under the guidance of expert academic supervisors.
  • Industry Placements: Gain real-world exposure through collaborations, internships, and applied projects.

 

By integrating rigorous research with experiential learning, the programme challenges you to push boundaries, explore innovative solutions, and make meaningful contributions to solving real-world engineering challenges — equipping you for leadership roles in academia, industry, and beyond.

NEXT: ENTRY REQUIREMENTS

Are you ready to take the next step in your academic journey? Our programme offers an exciting opportunity for individuals passionate about the field of Master of Science in Engineering. To ensure a smooth admission process, please take note of our entry requirements listed below.

Fulfilling one of the requirements listed below:

  1. Bachelor's Degree (Tier 1)
    Bachelor's Degree (Level 6 MQF) of computing or in the area of Science and Technology or related to computing, with a minimum CGPA of 2.75, OR

  2. Bachelor's Degree (Tier 2)
    Bachelor's Degree (Level 6 MQF) of computing or in the area of Science and Technology or related to computing, with CGPA below 2.75 but above 2.50, can be accepted subject to rigorous internal assessment process, OR

  3. Bachelor's Degree (Tier 3)
    Bachelor's Degree (Level 6 MQF) of computing or in the area of Science and Technology or related to computing, with CGPA less than 2.50 but above 2.00, with a minimum of 5 years working experience in a relevant field may be accepted.

Other equivalent qualification

Any other equivalent qualification recognized by Malaysian government or accepted by the Taylor's University Senate.

 

AND

 

Any of condition of the above would require student's research proposal to be approved.

 

International Students

IELTS 6.0 / TOEFL 60 (Internet Based Test) (May join IEN preparatory course and achieve the IELTS score) The validity of the IELTS or equivalent is within 2 years from the date of the examination.
