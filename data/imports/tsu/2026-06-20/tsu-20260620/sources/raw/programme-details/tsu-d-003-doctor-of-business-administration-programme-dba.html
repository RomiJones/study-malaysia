Overview

 

Designed for mid-level executives, senior leaders, and aspiring entrepreneurs, this programme offers an in-depth exploration of advanced business strategies, helping you elevate your strategic decision-making and leadership capabilities. Programme Structure

 

Combining guided coursework, action research, and networking opportunities through conferences and retreats, this programme equips learners with the advanced skills required to contribute to the advancement of business knowledge and practice. The initial stage focuses on building and sharpening knowledge of core business disciplines. Next, learners will explore research techniques to support their journey in developing and fine-tuning their research area. In the final stage, they will employ what they have learnt previously to contribute to new knowledge, method, and discoveries in the field of business.

        NEXT: ENTRY REQUIREMENTS

        Entry Requirements

         

        Are you ready to take the next step in your academic journey? Our programme offers an exciting opportunity for individuals passionate about the Doctor of Business Administration (DBA). To ensure a smooth admission process, please take note of our entry requirements listed below.

        Fulfilling one of the requirements listed below:

         

          i.     A Master’s degree (Level 7, MQF) in Business Administration, as accepted by the HEP Senate; OR

           

          ii.    A Master’s degree (Level 7, MQF) in non-related fields, accepted by the HEP Senate, subject to having relevant working experience and rigorous internal assessment; OR

           

          iii.   A Master’s degree (Level 7, MQF) in non-related fields, without relevant working experience, accepted by the HEP Senate, subject to passing prerequisite courses; OR

           

          iv.   Other qualifications equivalent to a Master’s degree (Level 7, MQF) recognised by the Malaysian Government.

          Accreditation of Prior Experiential Learning (APEL)

          The following criteria shall apply for working adults applying for Accreditation of Prior Experiential Learning (APEL):

          1. All applicants are subjected to appropriate APEL assessments conducted by the Malaysian Qualifications Agency (MQA)
          2. The APEL provision is only to be applied to Malaysian nationalities

           

           

          Working Experience

          Experience can be cumulative experience even before acquiring required qualification for a particular level of study. However, the experience must be relevant to the level of study as well as the content.

           

           

           

          Rigorous Internal Assessment

          Rigorous assessment to evaluate the suitability of an applicant for a programme through statement of purpose, interview and other methods may cover the following criteria: 

          • Demonstration of strong business experience, and that you have held significant managerial responsibilities.
          • Evidence of any achievements in your professional life, for example, consistently performing at a high level, consistent career progression and any impact that you have had on your organisation.
          • Professional skills that you have developed, for example, teamwork, leadership, problem-solving, negotiating and analytical skills.
          • How you will contribute to your class and what experiences you can bring to the institution. (Lancaster University, 2021)

           

           

          Prerequisite Courses

          The prerequisite courses must equip the applicant with necessary business knowledge of one level lower than the programme level:

          • Managing People
          • Managing Financial Resources
          • Understanding Customers
          • Understanding Environments and Strategic Planning

           

           

           

          International Students

          IELTS 6.0 (May join IEN preparatory course and achieve the IELTS score).

          Is Taylor's DBA offered online, in a hybrid format, or fully physical?

          Is Taylor's DBA offered online, in a hybrid format, or fully physical?

          Students are required to attend classes in person. In the final year, students will have greater flexibility to schedule sessions with their supervisor either online or in person.
          Can I maintain my employment while pursuing a DBA?

          Can I maintain my employment while pursuing a DBA?

          The Doctor of Business Administration (DBA) programme is designed to accommodate the schedules of working professionals. Many candidates study part-time and apply their learning directly to their professional roles.
          Why should I study Taylor's DBA?

          Why should I study Taylor's DBA?

          Taylor’s DBA prioritises in-person interaction for a richer learning experience, personalised coaching for tailored guidance, and experiential activities to build practical skills. This also fosters meaningful professional networking connections and a collaborative environment that might not be available solely online.   

           

          Ranked No. 1 Private University in Southeast Asia for Business and Management Studies (QS Subject Rankings 2023), Taylor’s offers a top-tier education that prepares you for business leadership. It is one of Malaysia’s top 5 private universities with AACSB accreditation, an accolade of academic excellence held by only 6% of business schools globally. We ensure a high-quality learning journey, supported by thorough assessments that cultivate excellence in every aspect.  
          Can attaining a DBA qualify me to become a professor?

          Can attaining a DBA qualify me to become a professor?

          Its rigorous curriculum, emphasis on research and publication, and teaching opportunities can equip you for a professorship. However, success requires continuous learning and progression through academic career pathways. 
          Can I apply for the DBA programme with a master's degree in a non-business-related field?

          Can I apply for the DBA programme with a master's degree in a non-business-related field?

          Yes, provided you meet the guidelines set forth by the Malaysian Qualifications Agency (MQA) and Taylor's Business School outlining the admission prerequisites. While a business background is ideal, strong academics, relevant work experience, and unique skills can qualify you. Kindly review the entry requirements to ensure eligibility and contact us for further assistance.
          What is the difference between a DBA and a PhD in Business?

          What is the difference between a DBA and a PhD in Business?

          DBA focuses on applying research in practical ways to solve real-world business problems, while the PhD in Business is more theoretical, preparing graduates for academic careers and advancing business knowledge. 
          How does action research and coursework mode benefit me?

          How does action research and coursework mode benefit me?

          Action research and coursework offer practical learning whereby you apply theory to real life, as well as a strong knowledge base through coursework. This flexible approach allows you to engage in learning activities at your own pace and network with peers through projects, ultimately advancing your career. 
          How are students in the DBA programme evaluated and assessed?

          How are students in the DBA programme evaluated and assessed?

          DBA students are assessed through a combination of module-based assessment, research evaluations, supervisor feedback on research, and sometimes peer review of work. This ensures the well-rounded development of research and business expertise.

           
          How does participation in the DBA programme contribute to advancing my career?

          How does participation in the DBA programme contribute to advancing my career?

          It advances your career by developing strong leadership skills, cultivating an innovative entrepreneurial mindset, and equipping you with research expertise to tackle complex business challenges, influence strategic decisions, and contribute to the success and sustainability of your company. 

           
          What support services are available to students enrolled in the DBA programme?

          What support services are available to students enrolled in the DBA programme?

          Taylor’s DBA students have access to a range of support services: modern learning and research facilities, experienced lecturers, industry-leading guest speakers, research guidance, and academic/well-being resources like advisors, tutors, and mental health support.
