Bachelor of Business Analytics
(Online Mode)

Course ID
(N-DL/0410/6/0034)(07/30)(MQA/PA18176)
Campus
SEGi University
Level of Study
Bachelor Degree
Awarding Institution
Dual Award (University of Chichester & SEGi University)

The Bachelor of Business Analytics (Honours) is a fully online, flexible programme designed for individuals seeking to upskill, reskill, or start fresh in the world of data and business. This future-focused degree equips students to turn data into strategic decisions using real-world tools such as Excel, Power BI, SQL, Python, and R.

Throughout the programme, learners explore core business and management topics, while applying their skills through case studies and hands-on projects. With a self-paced structure and a curriculum tailored to industry needs, graduates are empowered to lead and make data-driven decisions in any business environment.

One Programme, Two Degrees
University of Chichester-SEGi University Dual Award Partnership

Students enrolled in this programme benefit from a Dual Award partnership with the University of Chichester. Upon graduation, students receive two degree certificates within the same period of study, one from SEGi University, Malaysia and one from the University of Chichester, United Kingdom.

The programme is assessed and moderated by the University of Chichester, ranked among the top universities in the UK for Teaching Quality in the National Student Survey and a Gold award recipient of the UK Teaching Excellence Framework. Students enjoy internationally benchmarked academic standards and quality assurance, without the need to study in the UK.

Career opportunities

Typical examples of job positions include: Business analysts, Data analysts, Market research analysts, Operations analysts, Data visualisation specialists, Business intelligence (BI) associates, E-commerce analysts. As graduates, you may also pursue relevant postgraduate qualifications such as master’s programmes in business analytics, data science, or digital business, or undertake professional certifications from industry-recognised bodies including Certified Analytics Professional (CAP), Microsoft Power BI, or Google Data Analytics.
