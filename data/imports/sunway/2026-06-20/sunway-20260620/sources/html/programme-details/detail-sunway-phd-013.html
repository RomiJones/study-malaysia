Doctor of Business Administration

Overview

Hone your ability to generate original insights and frameworks that influence both academic knowledge and business practice. Develop skills to address pressing challenges in sustainability, digitalisation, and global competitiveness through applied research, and gain recognition as a scholar-practitioner. 

Learn from industry professionals with C-suite experience, many of whom sit on Sunway Business School’s Industry Advisory Board.

Students from this programme will be able to access Harvard Business Publishing Education resources for real-world insights, and study at Malaysia’s first non-government linked private university accredited by AACSB, the global gold standard held by less than 6% of business schools worldwide. Sunway Business School is also a member of the Principles for Responsible Management Education (PRIME), the European Foundation for Management Development (EFMD), and the Business Graduates Association.

Application Procedure

Step 1: Check to ensure you understand the programme and intakes. If you have any enquiries regarding the programme, see if there is any upcoming preview session you can join, or contact @email

Step 2: Compile your documents for an eligibility check and send it via our Eligibility Check portal. We will let you know the outcome via email within 7 working days.

Step 3: Once you have passed the eligibility check, we will be in touch with you for an interview arrangement

Step 4: Once you have passed the interview, you may proceed to enrol at https://onlineapplication.sunway.edu.my   

 

Eligibility Check

For us to advise on your eligibility for the programme, please submit the following documents at our Eligibility Check webpage.

Documents Required:

  1. Master’s degree official transcript 
  2. Master’s degree completion certificate 
  3. Bachelor's degree official transcript  
  4. Bachelor's degree official completion certificate  
  5. Diploma official transcript (if applicable)   
  6. Diploma completion certificate (if applicable)   
  7. Updated CV/Resume    
  8. IELTS (if applicable)
