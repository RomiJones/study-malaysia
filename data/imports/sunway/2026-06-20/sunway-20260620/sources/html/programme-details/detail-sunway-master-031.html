Qualify with APEL.A

Accreditation of Prior Experiential Learning for Access (APEL.A) T-7 is catered to individuals with work experience who want to pursue a master’s qualification, but do not possess a bachelor’s degree. 

APEL.A evaluates the experiential learning gained from formal, informal, and non-formal learning to determine an individual’s readiness to join a programme of study – transforming your job experience into an official qualification for entry. Details on APEL.A can be found here.

Eligibility requirements include:

  • Malaysian citizen
  • At least 30 years old
  • Possess at least an STPM/Diploma/equivalent qualification
  • Possess relevant work experience

Academic Requirement

A bachelor’s degree in related fields with a minimum CGPA of 2.75, or equivalent qualifications as accepted by the HEP Senate.

OR

A bachelor’s degree in related fields with a minimum CGPA of 2.50 and not meeting 2.75, with at least five years of relevant work experience.

OR

The completion of a prerequisite subject specified by the University for those with a bachelor’s degree in non-related fields with a minimum CGPA of 2.50 as accepted by the HEP Senate.

OR

An APEL.A Certification (T-7) (Recognition of Prior Learning).

English Language Requirement

Applicants who are not from countries that use English as the official language or whose academic qualifications were not attained in the English medium will be required to provide evidence of language ability via an IELTS result (minimum score of 6.5) or equivalent, or a minimum Band 4.5 in the Malaysian University English Test or equivalent to Common European Framework of Reference (High B2).

ONLINE MASTER OF FINANCE FAQs
Is a Master of Finance worthwhile? ▾

Yes. A Master of Finance is a valuable investment in your career, particularly if you aim to advance in corporate or global finance sectors. The financial sector contributed about 6.7% to Malaysia’s GDP in 2023, and is projected to grow by 4.3% in 2024.  Additionally, Master of Finance graduates in East and Southeast Asia have the second highest median starting salary after those in the United States, according to the Corporate Recruiters Survey 2023. A Master of Finance is not only key to a competitive salary, but it is also a pathway to greater employment opportunities in Malaysia.

Why should I pursue a Master of Finance? ▾

Pursuing a Master of Finance provides you with the opportunity to develop specialised financial expertise in areas like corporate finance, personal finance, investing, and financial technology.

The programme not only sharpens your analytical and decision-making skills but also equips you with tools and techniques to understand the global market.

Whether you’re looking to advance to a more senior financial role or take on a leadership role, the Master of Finance will empower you to excel in a rapidly changing industry.

How long does it take to complete a Master of Finance? ▾

Sunway University Online’s Master of Finance is designed to fit into your busy schedule, and can be completed in up to 24 months on a part-time basis. Depending on your needs, you can take one subject per seven-week semester.

Furthermore, there are multiple intake points throughout the year, allowing you the freedom to begin your studies at a time that suits you.

What kind of financial knowledge and skills will I gain with a Master of Finance? ▾

You will gain advanced knowledge in core areas of finance, including corporate finance, investing, financial statement analysis, and global financial markets.

Additionally, you will develop strong quantitative and qualitative analytical skills, allowing you to optimise financial resources effectively.

You will also learn how to integrate emerging technologies into your financial strategies by exploring financial technology and sustainability trends.

Besides that, you will be taught to use advanced hedging tools and techniques to shape your global perspectives and make informed decisions.

How will a Master of Finance improve my ability to explain complex financial concepts? ▾

The Master of Finance programme enhances your ability to clearly articulate complex financial concepts by combining case study examinations with practical, real-world applications.

As a result, you will learn to synthesise and present the data to communicate financial strategies and insights, and in a way that is accessible and actionable for diverse stakeholders.

What outcomes can I expect from the programme? ▾

Graduates of the Sunway University Online Master of Finance will develop advanced expertise in corporate finance, personal finance, and investing.

You will learn to investigate complex financial issues using cutting-edge theories and propose innovative, data-driven solutions to real-world challenges. Besides that, you will be able to analyse and interpret data with financial analytical tools, and hone your communication skills to synthesise strategies into clear messages for diverse audiences.

Additionally, you will acquire strong interpersonal and leadership skills, enabling you to adapt to dynamic team environments and guide financial decisions that incorporate ethical and professional standards.

By the end of the programme, you will be prepared to lead in the global financial sector, driving business growth through entrepreneurial strategies and evidence-based decision-making.

What jobs can I land with a Master of Finance? ▾

Whether you aim to specialise in investment management, financial planning, or corporate finance, a Master of Finance equips you with the skills needed to succeed in leadership positions. There are a wide range of high-level roles in the financial sector you can pursue, including Corporate Finance Manager, Investment Analyst, Financial Consultant, and Financial Advisor.

Why study the Master of Finance at Sunway over other universities? ▾

Our Master of Finance stands out with its combination of industry-recognised reputability , flexibility, and robust network.

Sunway University is ranked the #1 Private University in ASEAN (AppliedHE Private University Ranking ASEAN 2024), meaning you will be graduating from a top-ranked and widely recognised institution.

The programme is delivered fully online and available for part-time study, giving you the flexibility to study while balancing your professional and personal commitments.

Additionally, you can customise your programme according to your desired area of focus by selecting from a range of electives. On top of that, you will be able to make valuable connections through Sunway University’s industry-seasoned faculty and esteemed alumni network.
