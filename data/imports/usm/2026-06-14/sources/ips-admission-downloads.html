Student Visa


Download visa application guideline: Click here

International students are required to obtain Student Pass/Visa approval before can be allowed to register as USM student.

Application for Visa Approval is required to be completed 3 months before the new semester started (for Coursework/Mixed mode programs), or 3 months before the intended registration date (for Research mode programs).

Liaise with USM Visa Office team for assistance in visa matters.
