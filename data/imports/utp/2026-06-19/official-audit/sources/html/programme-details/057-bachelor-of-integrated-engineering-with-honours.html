Department Chair's Foreword

Dr. Zahiraniza Mustaffa
Chair, Department of Integrated Engineering

Welcome to Malaysia's first Department of Integrated Engineering - a place where conventional engineering silos dissolve and possibilities multiply.

In an increasingly complex and interconnected world, the ability to think beyond traditional boundaries has become essential. It demands engineers who can fluently navigate across mechanical gears, electronic signals, chemical reactions, data streams, and human needs. The community calls for bold thinkers, agile problem-solvers, and visionary collaborators. At the Department of Integrated Engineering, we cultivate the versatile engineers for the future.

Aligned with UTP's vision to be at the forefront of technology education and research, our department plays a critical role in nurturing integrated engineers - professionals who are future-focused, innovation-driven, and capable of bridging engineering knowledge with emerging technologies, sustainable practices, and human-centred solutions. We are deeply grounded in core engineering principles yet empowered to connect dots across disciplines, industries, and technologies. Our graduates don't just adapt to the future - they shape it.

Our academic and research approach embraces cross-disciplinary learning - whether in sustainable infrastructure, smart manufacturing, digital twins, or systems integration, our students are equipped to lead with both depth and breadth. Through an integrated curriculum, cutting-edge digital tools, and real-world project experiences in our Living Labs, students gain more than just technical expertise - they build a mindset for innovation, sustainability, and impact.

We foster an environment that encourages curiosity, adaptability, and ethical leadership. Through collaboration with academia, industry, and communities, our department advances UTP's mission to contribute to a more inclusive, resilient, and sustainable future. We welcome students, researchers, and partners to join us in redefining the future of engineering - integrated, impactful, and inspired.

[AP Ir Dr. Zahiraniza Mustaffa]
Chair, Department of Integrated Engineering
