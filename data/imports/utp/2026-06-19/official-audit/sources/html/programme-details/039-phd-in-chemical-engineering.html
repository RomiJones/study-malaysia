​Doctor of Phi​losophy​ (Ph.D.) in Chemical Engineering
(R3/524/8/0018) (12/29) (A8418)

Introduction
Our PhD programme by research in Chemical Engineering is designed to provide a dynamic and stimulating research environment, fostering innovation and excellence in various chemical engineering-related fields. 

As a research student, you will have the opportunity to work alongside leading experts and contribute to cutting-edge research projects that address some of the most pressing challenges in the industry. Our state-of-the-art facilities and interdisciplinary approach ensure that you gain comprehensive knowledge and hands-on experience in areas such as process engineering, materials science, environmental engineering, and biochemical engineering. 

Our PhD programme is tailored to support your academic and professional growth, preparing you for a successful career in academia, industry, or research institutions. Join us and become part of a vibrant community dedicated to advancing the frontiers of chemical engineering.

Programme Objectives

1. Experts who are able to practice the knowledge acquired, conduct independent research and generate innovative and creative solution for research and scholarly activities in chemical engineering related fields. 

2. Experts who promotes lifelong learning and are able to contribute to knowledge creation, innovation and creative solutions for the well-being and betterment of societies.

Programme Outcomes

  1. Demonstrate continuing and advanced knowledge and have the capabilities to further develop or use these in new situation or multi-disciplinary contexts
  2.  Analyse and evaluate problems in the Engineering discipline critically particularly in situations with limited information.
  3. Appraise available information and research evidence and apply it in the Engineering context.
  4. Plan and perform research undertakings professionally, ethically and responsibly
  5.  Report technical findings in both written and oral forms
  6. Recognise the needs for continuing professional development
  7.  Evaluate problems and provide solutions through the application of appropriate tools and techniques in Engineering.​​

​

Programme Highlights​

​
Main key focus areas (but not limited to): 
1. Process Monitoring, Fault Detection, and Diagnosis 
2. Process Modelling, Control, and Simulation 
3. Carbon Footprint Assessment, Sustainability Assessment, and Process Optimization 
4. Ionic Liquids for Chemical Processes, Surfactant Technology, green extraction, and energy applications 
5. Gas Hydrate for Carbon Capture & Storage, Desalination, and Flow Assurance 
6. Advanced Functional Nanomaterials 
7. CO2 Capture, Utilization, and Catalytic Reforming 
8. Biomass Processing and Biofuel Production 
9. Waste-to-Energy and Waste-to-Resources 
10. Hydrogen Production

Programme Details

Department:  Chemical Engineering Department

Intakes: January, May, and September

Mode of Deliverance & Mode of Study: Full time and Part time research

Course Duration:
Full time: 36 months (3 years) - 96 months (8 years)
Part time: 48 months (4 years) - 120 months (10 years)

Entry Requirements

1. Candidates must hold a Master Degree* in the relevant field OR equivalent from a recognized university. 

2. Bachelor’s Degree with first class OR minimum final CGPA 3.65/4.00 or its equivalent field from recognised university / institution & pass UTP internal rigorous assessment. 

3. Candidates to enrol in Engineering programmes but having qualification from different disciplines must undergo TWO pre-requisite courses. (Principles of Chemical Engineering and Process Safety and Loss Prevention).

​English Requirements​

Pass English requirement with a minimum score of: · 

  •  Engineering Programmes - IELTS - 5.0, TOEFL - 500 
  • Science/PhD. in Management/PhD. in Social Science & Humanities Programmes – IELTS – 6.0, TOEFL - 550 
  • Information Technology / Information Systems Programmes - IELTS – 6.0, TOEFL - 550 


However, this requirement is waived if candidate; 

  • have obtained Bachelor / Master or other relevant degree from Malaysian recognised institution whereby all courses are fully conducted in English or; 
  • a native of an English speaking countries or; 
  • graduated from English speaking countries institutions

Career Prospects

A PhD opens up advanced career opportunities such as: 

  • Academic roles as university professors or researchers 
  • Senior R&D positions in industries such as Oil & Gas & Energy Sector, processing industry, environmental & sustainability, materials, pharmaceutical, Safety, etc. 
  • Leadership roles in engineering consultancy firms · 
  • Entrepreneurial ventures in innovative chemical engineering technologies or solutions. 

PhD graduates are also highly sought after for their problem-solving, analytical, and technical expertise.

FAQs

1. How do I register for the programme? 
You can register through UTP Registry Admission. Detailed guidelines and application procedures are available on the UTP website. Click here: UTP REGISTRY - ADMISSION

2. How do I find a topic and a potential supervisor? 
Interested applicants can contact prospective supervisors from Chemical Engineering Department (see Staff Directories) with proposed topics, based on the supervisor expert area or institution’s focus area. 
Click here: Directories staff 

Research focus area: 
· Process monitoring, fault detection and diagnosis, Process modelling, control and simulation, Process Intensification 
· Carbon capture, utilization and storage, contaminants capture 
· Materials development for various applications
· Separations Processes and Reactions 
· Biomass and biochemical processing 
· Renewable energy 
· Hydrogen production and storage 
Students often align their research with the expertise of their chosen supervisor or the institution's focus areas. 

3. What is the tuition fee for the PhD programme? 
The tuition fee structure varies for local and international students. You can check the latest fee details on the UTP official website under the Postgraduate Fees section. Click here:
  • Tuition fees - Postgraduate Local Applicants
  • Tuition fees - Postgraduate International Applicants​​

4. Are there any financial aid options available? 
Yes, UTP offers  research assistantships, and industry-sponsored funding. Applicants can explore financial aid opportunities under​​ Financial Aid section on the UTP website.
