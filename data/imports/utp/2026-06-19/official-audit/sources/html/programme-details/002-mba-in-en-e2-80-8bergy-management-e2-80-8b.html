Master of Business Administration in Energy Management

Master of Business Administration in Energy Management

(JPT/BPP(R2/0414/7/0391)07/28)(MQA/FA2994)

​​Introduction

Rising energy demand is at odds with the threat of fast depleting energy resources—demanding energy ​providers to engage a strategic approach to energy management. Amid intensifying scrutiny over costs, carbon ou​​tput and sustainability, the oil, gas and energy industry requires a resourceful talent pool of energy management professionals to navigate global challenges in support of UN's Sustainable Development Goals.

In response to the global forces at work, Master of Business Administration in Energy Management (MBA-EM) prep​ares students to pivot to new technologies and drive towards a model that supports future-looking strategies. As such, the programme exposes students to a wide array of real industry challenges and case studies. In addition to lectures, the programme offers multi-sided learning approaches such as seminars and consultancy based projects. Ultimately, by leveraging the latest energy management knowledge and trends, students are poised to play a critical role in the design of the industry's carbon-neutral future.

The MBA-EM programme runs for 5 trimesters, 47​ credit hours, 20 months with a comprehensive curriculum design. The programme delivered via conventional face-to-face method​, comprises Busin​ess Core modules, Energy Management modules and a Management Consultancy Project.


​

Programme Objectives

  1. Specialists who can demonstrate the mastery of theoretical & practical knowledge in business.
  2. Specialists who can demonstrate comprehensive managerial and entrepreneurial skills to lead effectively and responsibly in organizations.
  3. Specialists who have abilities to adopt and apply digital applications and analytical techniques competently to support business functions.
  4. Specialists who have abilities to demonstrate teamwork, interpersonal skills, creativity and innovation skills.
  5. Specialists who are committed to learning for continuous development. 

Programme Outcomes

  1. Critically evaluate theories and concepts in business.
  2. Resolve business problems by providing recommendations to organisations.
  3. Organize critical business problems in organisations by adhering to legal, ethical and professional practices.
  4. Effectively communicate business solutions in written and oral forms and demonstrate effective collaborative skills with diverse stakeholders.
  5. Demonstrate abilities in analytics and digital skills to support business decision making. 
  6. Interpret and synthesis data to offer meaningful conclusion to support decision making in business.
  7. Demonstrate leadership skills and responsibility in managing business and energy related organizations.
  8. Demonstrate the skills and philosophies of lifelong learning in their academic and career development.
  9. Apply entrepreneurial skills in resolving business solutions.

Programme Highlights​

  1. ​​​MBA programme with specialization in Energy Management.
  2. Focusing on Energy Management, ESG (environmental, social, and governance), LCE (low-carbon economy), and sustainability.
  3. The programme also offers Data Analytics course to equip students with the ability/skill for interpreting data to get actionable insights for business decisions making.

Programme Details

Department: Management & Humanities Department​ 

Intakes: January, May, and September

Mode of Deliverance:

Modular
Min 1 year 8 months, Max 3 years
Full Time ODL


Entry Requirements
  • Bachelor’s Engineering Degree or any relevant field from a recognised university with a CGPA of 2.50 - 2.74 or its equivalent OR;
  • Bachelor’s Engineering Degree or any relevant field from a recognised university with a CGPA of 2.00 - 2.49 or its equivalent will require 5 years of working experience and internal rigorous assessment

Apel.A field:
  • Energy Management​

English Requirements
  • A minimum TOEFL score of 550 or equivalent
  • A minimum IELTS score of 6.0 or equivalent
  • Native English speakers or holding a degree with English as the medium of instruction may be exempted from this requirement

Pre-requisite courses

​Bachelor’s Degree from non-engineering relevant will have to take pre-requisite courses as below:

  • Management and Organisational Behaviour
  • Forecasting Methods for Management

It will be conducted 100% online

FAQs

1. Will the test and final exam be conducted online?
Yes, all test and final exam will be conducted online via UlearnX on weekend.

2. Do I need to come to UTP campus?
No, all ODL students will not need to come to UTP campus throughout their study. However, we might see each other in various engagements held in Kuala Lumpur.

3. What is the added value for MBA-EM at UTP?
There will be a session with Adjunct lecturers from Industry. We also have an Adjunct Professor talk with MBA-EM annually and a Visiting Professor to co-teach in certain subjects.
