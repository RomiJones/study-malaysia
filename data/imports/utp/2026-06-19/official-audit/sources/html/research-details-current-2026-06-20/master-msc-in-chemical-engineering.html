​MSc in Chemical Engineering
(JPT/BPP(R3/524/7/0008)02/29)(A7925)


Introduction
The MSc in Chemical Engineering by Research, offered by the Department of Chemical Engineering at Universiti Teknologi PETRONAS (UTP), Malaysia, is designed to provide a dynamic and stimulating research environment, fostering innovation and excellence in various chemical engineering-related fields.

As a research student, you will have the opportunity to work alongside leading experts and contribute to cutting-edge research projects that address some of the most pressing challenges in the industry. Our state-of-the-art facilities and interdisciplinary approach ensure you gain comprehensive knowledge and hands-on experience in various chemical engineering-related research areas, such as chemical processes, materials science, environmental engineering, and biochemical engineering.

Our program is tailored to support your academic and professional growth, preparing you for a successful career in academia, industry, or research institutions. Join us and become part of a vibrant community dedicated to advancing the frontiers of chemical engineering.



Programme Objectives

1. Specialists who are able to practice the knowledge acquired and generate innovative and creative solutions for research and scholarly activities in chemical engineering related fields. 

2. Specialists who promote lifelong learning and are able to contribute to knowledge creation, innovation and creative solutions for the well-being and betterment of societies.​

Programme Outcomes

  1. Demonstrate mastery of knowledge in chemical engineering related fields.
  2. Apply analytical skills in chemical engineering related fields.
  3.  Relate ideas to technological or business or societal issues in chemical engineering related fields.
  4. Conduct research with minimal supervision and adhere to ethical and professional standards.
  5.  De​​velop leadership qualities through communicating and working effectively with peers and stakeholders
  6. Evaluate problem and generate solutions to problems using advanced chemical engineering knowledge and critical thinking skills
  7. Analyse and evaluate information for lifelong learning.​

Programme Highlights​

​
Main key focus areas (but not limited to): 
· Process Monitoring, Fault Detection, and Diagnosis 
· Process Modelling, Control, and Simulation
· Carbon Footprint Assessment, Sustainability Assessment, and Process Optimization 
· Ionic Liquids for Chemical Processes, Surfactant Technology, green extraction, and energy applications 
· Gas Hydrate for Carbon Capture & Storage, Desalination, and Flow Assurance 
· Advanced Functional Nanomaterials 
· CO2 Capture, Utilization, and Catalytic Reforming 
· Biomass Processing and Biofuel Production ​
· Waste-to-Energy and Waste-to-Resources 
· Hydrogen Production

Programme Details

Department:  Chemical Engineering Department

Intakes: January, May, and September

Mode of Delivery: 

The Chemical Engineering by Research programme at UTP is designed to provide a flexible and research-intensive learning environment. Below are the key modes of deliverance: 

1. Research-Centric Approach 

2. One-on-One Supervision 

3. Access to State-of-the-Art Facilities 

4. Workshops and Seminars 

5. Online and On-Campus Support 

6. Collaborative and Industry-Driven Research 

7. Flexible Study Plan


​Mode of Study and Duration: 

Full Time: Min: 2 Years Max: 4 Years 

Part Time: Min 3 Years Max: 6 Years


Entry Requirements

1. Candidates must hold a BSc Degree in Engineering/ Engineering Technology discipline with minimum CGPA of 2.50/4.00 or equivalent in the relevant field from a recognised university. 
2. Candidates with BSc Degree in Engineering/Engineering Technology discipline with minimum CGPA 2.00 but below 2.50, can be admitted and must have 5 years working experience & pass UTP internal rigorous assessment.
3. Candidates to enrol in Engineering programmes but having qualification from different disciplines must undergo pre-requisite courses.

​​​English Requirements​

Pass English requirement with a minimum score of: 

  •  Engineering Programmes - IELTS - 5.0, TOEFL - 500 
  •  Science/PhD. in Management/PhD. in Social Science & Humanities Programmes – IELTS – 6.0, TOEFL - 550 
  •  Information Technology / Information Systems Programmes - IELTS – 6.0, TOEFL - 550 

However, this requirement is waived if candidate; 

  • have obtained Bachelor / Master or other relevant degree from Malaysian recognised institution whereby all courses are fully conducted in English or; 
  • a native of an English speaking countries or;
  • graduated from English speaking countries institutions​

​Pre-Requisite- Courses

​Bachelor’s Degree from non-engineering relevant will have to take pre-requisite courses as below: 

• Principles of Chemical Engineering 

• Process Safety and Loss Prevention 

It will be conducted 100% online

How will it improve my career prospects?

 Apply the principles of chemical engineering to health, energy, food, environment, medicine, petrochemicals and pharmaceuticals in the private and public sectors.​

Career Prospects

Graduates can pursue careers in: 

  • Advanced positions in Process Engineering, Product Development, Quality Assurance, Control Engineer, Environmental Engineering 
  • R&D in engineering industries 
  • Academic roles as lecturers or researchers 
  • Technical Consultant & Project Manager, providing expert advice to companies on chemical engineering-related projects and challenges. 
  • Entrepreneurship, Startup Founder, based on innovative chemical engineering technologies or solutions. 

Many graduates also use the MSc as a stepping stone to PhD studies.

FAQs

1. What is the difference between a research-based MSc and a taught MSc programme? 
A research-based MSc focuses on independent research under the guidance of a supervisor, culminating in a thesis. Unlike taught programs, it has minimal coursework (only Research Methodology) and emphasizes the development of critical thinking, problem-solving, and technical skills through research. 

2. How do I register for the programme? 
You can register through UTP Registry Admission. Detailed guidelines and application procedures are available on the UTP website. 
Click here: UTP REGISTRY - ADMISSION 

3. What kind of research topics can I pursue during the MSc programme? 
Research areas include but are not limited to: 
· Process monitoring, fault detection and diagnosis, Process modelling, control and simulation, Process Intensification 
· Carbon capture, utilization and storage, contaminants capture 
· Materials development for various applications 
· Separations Processes and Reactions 
· Biomass and biochemical processing 
· Renewable energy 
· Hydrogen production and storage 

Students often align their research with the expertise of their chosen supervisor or the institution's focus areas.

4. How do I find a potential supervisor? 
Interested applicants can contact prospective supervisors from Chemical Engineering Department (see Staff Directories) with proposed topics, based on the supervisor expert area or institution’s focus area. 
Click here: Directories staff ​

5. Are there any financial aid options available? 
Yes, UTP offers  research assistantships, and industry-sponsored funding. Applicants can explore financial aid opportunities under​ Financial Aid section on the UTP website.
