Doctor of Phi​losophy​ (Ph.D.) ​in Petroleum Geoscience

(R3/0532/8/0001)​ (12/27) (A8319)​

​​​​
Introduction
A broad range of technical fields associated with hydrocarbon exploration and production to satisfy your intellectual curiosity. Use this advanced degree as a prelude to a career in the petroleum industry​

Programme Objectives

To produce doctoral graduates who are:

  1. Competent in research, modeling, systems analysis and development of petroleum geosciences at an advanced level, contributing substantially to the development and generation of new techniques, ideas or approaches and then apply their knowledge.
  2. Able to make informed judgments on complex issues in petroleum geosciences and an innovative approach to tackling and solving problems.


​

Programme Outcomes 


To produce petroleum geosciences doctoral graduates who demonstrate the ability:
1. To create and interpret new knowledge, through original research, of a quality to satisfy peer review, extend the forefront of the discipline, and merit publication.
2. To apply a systematic acquisition and understanding of a substantial body of knowledge that is at the forefront of field of petroleum geosciences academically and/or in professional practice.
3. To lead the nation’s petroleum geosciences sector and drive the development of its petroleum industry.
4. To conduct lifelong learning for professional development.
5. To uphold good practices and professional conduct.

Programme Highlights


Core Specialis​ation:
• Rock Physics
• Advanced Seismic Technology
• Non- Seismic Methods
• Integrated Basin Analysis
• Reservoir Characterisation, Modelling & Simulation
• Carbonate Reservoir Characterisation
• Carbonate Sedimentology
• Diagenesis & Sequence Stratigraphy
• Subsurface Storage
• Subsurface Resources and Energy
• Digital Geoscience

Certification: PaleoScan, Drone Mapping, PETREL and many
Software: GeoSoftware: Hampson-Russel, PaleoScanTM software, SLB PETROMOD, PETREL, ArcGIS, IGI'S Geochemical Software, and many more
Industry Collaboration: PETRONAS, Beicipe, SLB, Halliburton is main 
Others:


Programme Details

Department: Department of Geoscience

Intakes: January, May, and September

Mode of Deliverance:

Full Time: 3 to 8 years
Part Time: 4 to 10 years


Entry Requirements:

i. A Master's Degree; or
ii. Other qualifications equivalent to a Master's Degree that are accepted by the HEP Senate.​
iii. Candidates without a related qualification in the field/s or working experience
in the relevant fields must undergo appropriate prerequisite courses
determined by the HEP​


Research Attachment Opportunity  

• As per project

Career Prospects  

• Geoscientist, Data Science, Digitalization, Geo-modeller, Geophysicist ​
