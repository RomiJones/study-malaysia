​​Entry Requirement

​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​Qualification for Each Programme
 
​​i) Academic Requirement​
 
Master Programme (by Research)​​

 Bachelor Degree


Engineering
  1. Candidates must hold a BSc Degree in Engineering/ Engineering Technology discipline with a minimum CGPA of 2.50/4.00 or equivalent in the relevant field from a recognized university.
  2. Candidates with BSc Degree in Engineering/Engineering Technology discipline with a minimum CGPA of 2.00 but below 2.50,​ can be admitted and must have 5 years of working experience & pass UTP internal rigorous assessment.
  3. Candidates with BSc Degree qualification from a different discipline must undergo pre-requisite courses​ in Engineering/ Engineering Technology.​
Science & Social Science & Humanities


 
  1. Candidates must hold a BSc Degree with a minimum CGPA of 2.50/4.00 or equivalent in the relevant field from a recognized university.
  2. Candidates with BSc Degree with a minimum CGPA of 2.00 but below 2.50,​ can be admitted and must have 5 years of working experience & pass UTP internal rigorous assessment.​​
​Management​
  1. Candidates must hold a BSc Degree in Business Studies discipline with a minimum CGPA of 2.75/4.00, or equivalent in the relevant field from a recognized university.
  2. Candidates with BSc Degree in  Business Studies discipline with minimum CGPA of 2.50 but below 2.75,​ can be admitted and must pass UTP internal rigorous assessment.
  3. Candidates with BSc Degree in Business Studies discipline with minimum CGPA 2.00 but below 2.50,​ can be admitted and must have 5 years working experience & pass UTP internal rigorous assessment.​
Information Technology/ Information Systems
  1. Candidates must hold a BSc Degree in Computing discipline with minimum CGPA 3.00/4.00, or equivalent in the relevant field from a recognized university.
  2. Candidates with BSc Degree in Computing discipline with minimum CGPA 2.50 but below 3.00,​ can be admitted and must pass UTP internal rigorous assessment.
  3. Candidates with BSc Degree in Computing discipline with minimum CGPA 2.00 but below 2.50,​ can be admitted and must have 5 years working experience & pass UTP internal rigorous assessment.
  4. Candidates with BSc Degree qualification from different disciplines, must undergo pre-requisite courses​ in computing.
​ ​

 ​​

Master Programme 

(by Coursework & ODL)​​

​Bachelor Degree

Engineering
  1. Candidates must hold a BSc Degree in Engineering/ Engineering Technology discipline with minimum CGPA 2.50/4.00 or equivalent in the relevant field from a recognized university.
  2. Candidates with BSc Degree in Engineering/Engineering Technology discipline with minimum CGPA 2.00 but below 2.50,​ can be admitted and must have 5 years working experience & pass UTP internal rigorous assessment.
  3. Candidates with BSc Degree qualification from different discipline, must undergo pre-requisite courses​ in Engineering/ Engineering Technology.​
Petroleum Geoscience​
​​
  1. Candidates must hold a BSc Degree with minimum CGPA 2.50/4.00 or equivalent qualifications in the relevant field from a recognized university.
  2. Candidates with BSc Degree with minimum CGPA 2.00 but below 2.50,​ can be admitted and must have 5 years working experience & pass UTP internal rigorous assessment.​​
​Business Administration (Energy ​Management​)
  1. Candidates must hold a Bachelor Degree with minimum CGPA 2.50/4.00, or equivalent from a recognized university.
  2. Candidates with Bachelor Degree with minimum CGPA 2.00 but below 2.50,​ can be admitted, subject to passing UTP internal rigorous assessment.
  3. ​Candidates with Bachelor Degree qualification from different discipline, must undergo pre-requisite courses​ in Business.​
Applied Computing
  1. Candidates must hold a BSc Degree in Computing discipline with minimum CGPA 2.75/4.00, or equivalent in the relevant field from a recognized university.
  2. Candidates with BSc Degree in Computing discipline with minimum CGPA 2.50 but below 2.75,​ can be admitted and must pass UTP internal rigorous assessment.
  3. Candidates with BSc Degree in Computing discipline with minimum CGPA 2.00 but below 2.50,​ can be admitted and must have 5 years working experience & pass UTP internal rigorous assessment.
  4. Candidates with BSc Degree qualification from different disciplines, must undergo pre-requisite courses​ in computing.

​​
 PhD Programme 
  1. ​​​​Candidates must hold a Master's Degree* in the relevant field OR equivalent from a recognized university.​​​
  2. Candidates to enrol  in Engineering/ Engineering Technology & Computing programmes but having  qualification from different disciplines must undergo pre-requisite courses.
*Master's degree must be equivalent to Malaysian Qualifications Framework, Level 7 Master’s Degree.​​
 
 ii) English Requirement
  
Pass the English requirement with a minimum score of:
  • Engineering Programmes -  IELTS - 5.0, TOEFL iBT/Essentials - 500 
  • Science/PhD. in Management/PhD. in Social Science & Humanities Programmes – IELTS –            6.0,  TOEFL iBT/Essentials - 550
  • Information Technology / Information Systems Programmes - IELTS – 6.0, TOEFL iBT/Essentials​ - 550

 However, this requirement is waived if:
  • ​have obtained Bachelor / Master or other relevant degrees from Malaysian recognized institution whereby all courses are fully conducted in English or;
  • a native of an English-speaking country or;
  • graduated from English-speaking countries institutions
