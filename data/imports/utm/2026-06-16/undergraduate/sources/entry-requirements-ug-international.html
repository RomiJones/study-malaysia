Entry requirements for university are formal criteria you must meet in order to be considered for a degree course you’re applying to. Check your result here if you are eligible for the desired course.

Entry Requirements
A Senior High School Certificate or equivalent pre – university qualifications. See more details for the entry requirements based on country; and
Pass the English Proficiency requirements; and
Pass the UTM interview (for Bachelor of Architecture & Bachelor of Science Industrial Design).
