Programme Structures, Features and Award Requirements

The programme is offered in full-time mode based on a two (2) Semester Academic Year with several courses being delivered and assessed in each Semester. Assessment: 30-50% examination, 50-70% course work. To graduate, students MUST:

  1. Achieve a total of 130 credit hours with minimum of CPA of 2.0.
  2. Pass industrial training (equivalent to 12 credit hours), which 4 credits will be graded & 8 credit as HW status.
  3. Complete Final Year Projects inclusive the dissertation thesis.
How To Apply?
  1. Application For Bachelor Degree Programme (for SPM  or STPM Graduates/ Equivalent)
  2. Application for Bachelor Degree Programme (Local & International Student)
  3. Result of admission for Bachelor Degree Programme (Local Student)
