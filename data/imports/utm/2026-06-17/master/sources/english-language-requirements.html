English Language Requirements

Applicants who do not meet the English proficiency requirements of their chosen programme at Universiti Teknologi Malaysia (UTM) can  improve their English at Intensive English Programme (IEP) at Language Academy, UTM or the Certified Intensive English Programme (CIEP) at ELS Language Centres in Malaysia.

More Information about IEP UTM

ELS Language Centres has been a trusted partner of UTM since 2015. The ELS Certified Intensive English Programme (CIEP) is approved by the Malaysian Ministry of Higher Education, allowing university admission without the need for IELTS, TOEFL, or PTE tests.
ELS is conveniently located in D07 (CICT Building) on the UTM Skudai campus, and in Kuala Lumpur near KLCC, just 2 km from the UTM KL campus. ELS also offers the flexibility to study online from your home country while you apply for your student visa.

More Information about CIEP ELS

Undergraduate

Candidates who will join UTM starting from September 2023 must meet one of the following English language requirements :

(Please note that English language test results are valid for two (2) years only. Expired test results will not be accepted.)

English Language Test 

Required Bands and Score for Entry to ALL Programme EXCEPT TESL Programme 

Required Bands and Scores for Entry to the Teaching English As A Second Language (TESL) Programme 
Malaysian University English Test (MUET) Band 4.0 and above  Band 5.0 and above 
International English Language Testing System (IELTS) Band 5.5 and above  Band 6.5 and above 
Test of English as a Foreign Language (TOEFL) iBT / TOEFL IBT Home Edition Score of 46 and above  Score of 79 and above 
The Pearson Test of English (PTE Academic / PTE Academic Online) Score of 51 and above  Score of 63 and above 
Cambridge English Qualification (CEQ) – B2 First (FCE), C1 Advanced (CAE), C2 Proficiency (CPE), Linguaskill Online Score of 160 and above  Score of 176 and above 
Certified Intensive English Programme (CIEP – ELS) Complete level 108 and above                     –

IMPORTANT NOTE :

Intensive  English Programme (IEP) and Certified 

Students who do not meet the English language proficiency standards or requirements are required to attend : 

  • Intensive English Programme (IEP) conducted by the UTM Language Academy and obtain a minimum score in MUET / IELTS / TOEFL / Pearson Test of English / Cambridge English Qualification, OR
  • Certified Intensive English Programme (CIEP) conducted by the ELS Language Centre and complete Level 108.

 

Cambridge Linguaskill Test

Linguaskill is a 100% online and computer-based test, which enables test takers to be remotely proctored and have their English competency levels tested wherever they are. However, we would like to inform you that UTM will only accept the Linguaskill Online Language proficiency test results if the candidate registers for the test with Malaysian test center specifically under SASBADI (Institution: MY959) which is the exclusive and authorized Linguaskill Agent by Cambridge Assessment English in Malaysia. The decision to use a VPN to register for the Linguaskill test with a Malaysian test center is up to the individual student.

* Candidates for Cambridge Linguaskill test make take the online test only if they are located in Malaysia, Singapore and Philippines. Candidates outside these three countries are not permitted to book and take the online test

Candidates may click the link below to register for the Cambridge Linguaskill test : https://e4c.sasbadi.com/linguaskill/register/ra-utm1-c1

OTHER EXEMPTION FOR UNDERGRADUATE INTERNATIONAL STUDENT :

1. Candidates from International Schools, A-level and International Baccalaureate qualifications are exempted from the UTM English language requirement.

2. Candidates from UTM Foundation or other Foundation programme in Malaysia are exempted from the UTM English language requirement.

OTHER EXEMPTION FOR UNDERGRADUATE INTERNATIONAL STUDENT

Candidates from International Schools, A-Level and International Baccalaureate qualifications are exempted from the UTM English language requirement.

OTHER EXEMPTION FOR POSTGRADUATE INTERNATIONAL STUDENT

Candidates from Indonesia, Brunei and other nations that use Malay as medium of communication are exempted from the UTM English language requirements based on the following conditions:

  • The candidate intends to register for an MPhil or a PhD programme by research.
  • The candidate declares that the thesis/dissertation will be written in Malay and approved by the supervisor

Candidates for Islamic Civilization Academy (Faculty of Social Sciences and Humanities) programme can use Bahasa Arab/Arabic as to write their thesis based on the following conditions:

  • The candidate intends to register for a MPhil or PhD programme by research
  • The candidate declares that the thesis/dissertation will be written in Arabic and approved by the supervisor
