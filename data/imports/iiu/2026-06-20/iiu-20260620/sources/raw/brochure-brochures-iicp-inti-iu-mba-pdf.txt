The Master of Business Administration (MBA) in collaboration with INTI
International University is designed to develop forward-thinking leaders with the 
skills and expertise to excel in todays fast-changing global business landscape.
The programme combines academic excellence with real-world relevance 
through Problem-Based Learning and Project-Based Learning approaches that
strengthen critical thinking, leadership, and strategic decision-making.
Students will explore a comprehensive range of management disciplines, gaining 
the knowledge and perspective to navigate complex business environments and 
make informed, high-impact decisions.
Offered in both full-time and part-time modes, this MBA provides the flexibility 
needed by working professionals while maintaining strong industry alignment and 
practical learning outcomes. 
Programme Structure

• Business Economics

• Business Research Methods

• Managing Information Systems
• Marketing Management

• Project
