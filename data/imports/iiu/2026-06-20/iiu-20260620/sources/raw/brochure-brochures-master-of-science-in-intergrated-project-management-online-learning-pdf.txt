MASTER OF SCIENCE IN
INTEGRATED PROJECT
MANAGEMENT
(Online Learning)
This programme develops students’ project 
management skills through practical assessments 
and activities, preparing graduates to become 
lifelong learners and future leaders in construction 
project management and related sectors.
With a strong focus on on-site project management 
across manufacturing, construction, operations, and 
consultancy, the programme offers hands-on, 
real-world experience. Open to students from 
engineering, IT, and technology-related backgrounds, 
the programme uses model-driven and project-based 
learning to equip graduates to make informed 
construction project management decisions that 
benefit their organisations.
Through real-world projects, selected online
readings, discussions, activities and assignments, 
we bring industry practices into the classroom. 
Students remain closely connected to real-world 
applications and stay informed about current and 
emerging project management issues, trends and 
challenges.
Upon completion, graduates will be able to address 
project management objectives by effectively
identifying and utilising organisational resources 
while integrating project management processes 
with the knowledge and skills gained throughout 
the programme.
ENTRY REQUIREMENTS:
i.   Bachelor’s Degree (Level 6 Malaysian Qualifications Framework, MQF) in a relevant field with a minimum CGPA of 2.50; OR
ii.  Bachelor’s Degree (Level 6 MQF) with a CGPA of above 2.00 but less than 2.50 may be admitted, subject to relevant working experience.
iii. Candidates without a qualification in the related fields or working experience in the relevant fields must undergo appropriate prerequisite courses determined 
     by the university and meet the minimum CGPA based on (i) to (ii).
English Language Requirements: Candidates are required to achieve a minimum score of 5.0 in IELTS, 500 in TOEFL or its equivalent.
