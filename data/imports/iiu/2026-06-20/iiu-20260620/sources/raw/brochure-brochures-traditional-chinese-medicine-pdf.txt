HEALING TOUCH
With a tradition stretching for hundreds of years, Traditional Chinese Medicine 
(TCM) incorporates a holistic approach to treating patients with a combination of 
acupuncture, dietary therapy, herbal medicine and more. INTI'S Bachelor of Traditional 
Chinese Medicine (Hons) degree prepares student to be fully trained medical 
practitioner, exposed to every aspect of TCM integrated with modern
medical knowledge and empowered with the right soft skills to succeed.
As Malaysia’s first fully accredited academic programme for Traditional Chinese 
Medicine, the programme is recognised by both the Malaysian Ministry of Health (MoH) 
and the Malaysian Qualifications Agency (MQA), enabling graduates to pursue careers 
in Malaysia and in other eligible countries.
ACHIEVE MASTERY OF MANDARIN AND ENGLISH - TWO OF THE WORLD’S MOST WIDELY 
SPOKEN LANGUAGES
The Bachelor of Traditional Chinese Medicine (Hons) is a bilingual programme that is 
conducted in both English and Mandarin with lectures and course materials conveyed 
in both languages. While a foundation in Mandarin is desirable, non-native speakers 
are able to undertake complementary elective courses at INTI to achieve a suitable 
level of fluency in Mandarin. Upon completion of the course, graduates will have 
achieved mastery of two of the world’s most widely spoken languages.
PRACTICAL HANDS-ON COMPONENT
A robust practical component is integrated into the academic programme. As part of 
their course requirements, students will follow a clinical supervisor to be exposed to 
standard medical practices in Traditional Chinese Medicine in a medical practice in 
Malaysia. Students will receive guidance in patient diagnosis and in learning various 
TCM treatments under supervision. Upon graduation, students will have completed 
many hours of clinical attachment and hands-on training.
This hands-on component is an invaluable foundation when graduates undertake 
their housemanship programme after graduation. To gain international exposure, and 
as part of their training, selected students will also be called upon to attend global 
symposiums and talks as well as participate in voluntary humanitarian efforts.
