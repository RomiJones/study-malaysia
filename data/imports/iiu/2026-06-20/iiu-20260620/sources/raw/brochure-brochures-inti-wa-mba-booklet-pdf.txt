WHAT IS APEL (C)?
ACCREDITATION of Prior Experiential Learning or APEL , is a systematic  process that involves the 
identiﬁcation, documentation and assessment of  prior experiential learning to determine the extent 
to which an individual  has achieved the desired learning outcomes, for access to a programme  of 
study and/or award of credits.
APEL provides an opportunity for individuals with working experience but lack formal academic 
qualiﬁcations to pursue studies at Higher Education Institutions. In general, knowledge obtained 
 through both formal education and work experience is assessed.
