MANAGEMENT
(Online Learning)
The Master of Science in Facilities Management 
(Online Learning) is designed to enhance and 
complement students' existing knowledge and 
experience in construction and facilities 
management through a flexible, learner-centred 
approach.
The programme incorporates a diverse range of 
assessments and practical activities to develop 
critical thinking, problem-solving, and 
decision-making skills, preparing graduates to 
become effective leaders and lifelong learners in 
the industry.
With a focus on key areas of facilities management, 
the curriculum integrates model-driven 
development with project-based learning. This 
equips graduates with the ability to make informed, 
strategic decisions that add value to organisational 
performance.
